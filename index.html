Pradeep Singh

Director

Mining Engineer and Alumnus of ISM Dhanbad with 25 years of experience in production, consulting, mine planning, and optimization. He started his career at MECON, India and was associated with various mine consulting projects in metal and coal mining. He moved to South Africa in year 2009 to work in Phosphate mine of FOSKOR Limited, South Africa. Working with various industries & geographies developed strong technical and managerial skills. For the past decade, he has successfully managed various businesses in India including port operation/stevedoring, manufacturing of FIBC, Operation & Maintenance business, trading & dredging. Recognized as a visionary strategist and trendsetter in the industry, he is known for his innovative approach and deep sector expertise
