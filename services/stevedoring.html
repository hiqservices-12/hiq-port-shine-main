Service Capability

Stevedoring & Cargo Handling

Efficient bulk cargo handling at port berths with optimised vessel turnaround.

Overview Projects Team

Positioning

What this capability delivers.

HiQ Services provides stevedoring and bulk cargo handling services at Visakhapatnam Port. Our operations are managed by professionals with deep port operating experience, using mechanised handling equipment to deliver reliable vessel turnaround and cargo integrity.

Scope

Scope of services.

  • 01

    Vessel stevedoring — loading and unloading of bulk cargo including coal, iron ore, fertilisers, minerals

  • 02

    Mechanised cargo handling — grab unloaders, conveyor systems, front-end loaders

  • 03

    Cargo receipt and tallying — quantity verification, quality sampling, damage reporting

  • 04

    Hatch inspection and pre-arrival planning — cargo stowage review, equipment pre-positioning

  • 05

    Cargo documentation — bill of lading verification, freight certificate preparation

  • 06

    Safety and compliance — ISPS compliance, port authority coordination, cargo segregation

Capability

Handling Capability

HiQ's stevedoring operations at Vizag are integrated with our EQ-1 terminal, giving cargo owners a seamless flow from vessel to storage to inland despatch. Our 2 × 2500 TPH grab ship unloaders are among the higher-capacity unloading systems at the port.
